Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Ledge is designed with privacy as a core principle. We believe your personal and business financial records belong strictly to you.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share your financial data on external servers. All ledger books, categories, transactions, and notes you enter into Ledge are stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

2. Tracking & Analytics

Ledge does not use third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral trackers, or cross-app tracking.

3. Face ID / Touch ID Security

If you enable App Lock, authentication is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework on your device. Ledge never accesses or stores your biometric data or device passcode.

4. Subscriptions & Payments

Optional Ledge Pro subscriptions are processed securely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive or store your credit card or payment details.

5. CSV Exports

When you choose to export your ledger to a CSV file, the file is generated locally on your device and shared only to the destination you explicitly select using the iOS Share Sheet.

6. Children's Privacy

Ledge does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at ledgeapp.support@gmail.com.
