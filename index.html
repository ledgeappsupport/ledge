Frequently Asked Questions

  • Where is my financial data stored? Your transactions stay privately on your iPhone. Ledge never sells or tracks your financial records.
  • How do I restore my Ledge Pro subscription? Open Ledge → Settings → Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID.
  • How do I cancel or manage my subscription? Open iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Ledge, or tap Manage Subscription inside Ledge Settings.
